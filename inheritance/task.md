### Employee Management System


* Create a parent class called Employee with private properties for name, employeeId, and salary. The class should provide methods to display employee details, retrieve the salary, and increase the salary by a given amount.

* Now create two child classes, Developer and Manager, that inherit from Employee.

* A Developer should have a programmingLanguage property and a method to indicate that the developer is writing code. A Manager should have a teamSize property and a method to indicate that the manager is conducting a meeting.

* Demonstrate encapsulation by keeping the employee properties private and demonstrate inheritance using extends and super().

* Create at least one Developer and one Manager object and demonstrate their details, salary operations, and respective activities in the console.

Bonus ⭐

Add a calculateBonus() method where:

A Developer receives 10% of their salary as a bonus.

A Manager receives 20% of their salary as a bonus.
